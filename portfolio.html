UX RESEARCH: USER INTERVIEW
Summary
Problem
Process
Research
Ideation
Design
Test
Conclusion

Scope of participants: Product managers, stylists and modelists
Number of interviews: 27 ( from 8 companies)
Supported tool: Dovetail
Data analysis approach: Atomic research
Obtained user insights:

Ineffective Annotation

The current annotation system used during meetings is inefficient and lacks clarity. It is unclear what each pin on the mannequin stands for an agreement or an option, leading to confusion among the participants when the meeting ends.
Additionally, the value of information noted in personal notes varies, making it difficult to transfer and determine the note which hold the most significance on the same mannequin that have the same pin/agreement. This lack of effective annotation hinders the decision-making process and slows down progress.

Overwhelming Number of Tools

The meetings require the use of multiple tools and resources, which adds complexity and slows down the workflow.
Participants need to manipulate various sampling tissues or garments, manage multiple mannequins, and work with different operating systems. This abundance of tools increases the chances of errors, wastes time in transitioning between tools, and creates a barrier to seamless collaboration.

Misunderstanding in Video Conferences

The fashion industry relies heavily on international collaborations, and conducting meetings through video conferences is a common practice.
However, there have been recurring instances of miscommunication during these conferences, leading to misunderstandings. Factors such as language barriers, cultural differences, and technical difficulties contribute to ineffective communication, which hampers the decision-making process and impedes progress.

CLOTHING PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT LIFE CYCLE
Summary

A macro view of the fashion design process involving stylist, modelist and project manager
Research
  • Conduct market research to identify current fashion trends and consumer preferences.
  • Gather inspiration from various sources such as fashion magazines, blogs, social media, and runway shows.
  • Explore different fabrics, materials, colors, and patterns to incorporate into designs.
  • Brainstorm and sketch initial design ideas based on research findings.
Design
  • Create detailed sketches or digital mock-ups of clothing designs.
  • Collaborate with the design team to refine and finalize the designs.
  • Consider factors such as target audience, season, and brand guidelines while designing.
  • Make decisions on the type of fabrics, trims, and embellishments to be used.
Material Sourcing
  • Communicate with suppliers to source the required fabrics and materials for production.
  • Evaluate and compare different suppliers based on quality, cost, and availability.
  • Coordinate with the production team to ensure timely delivery of materials.
Prototype Development
  • Work with pattern makers and sample makers to create prototypes of the designs.
  • Provide feedback and make necessary adjustments to improve the fit, aesthetics, and functionality of the garments.
  • Imply developable/potential accessories to each product line
  • Define the potential product series/ product life circle
  • Conduct fittings and make further modifications to ensure the designs meet the desired standards.
Production Planning
  • Collaborate with the production team to determine the quantity and timeline for production.
  • Monitor the production process to ensure adherence to quality standards and timely delivery.
  • Address any issues or delays that arise during production.
Marketing & Promotion
  • Coordinate with the marketing team to plan and execute promotional campaigns for the product line.
  • Participate in photoshoots, fashion shows, and other marketing events to showcase the designs.
  • Provide input and guidance on branding, packaging, and visual merchandising
Marketing & Promotion
  • Monitor sales performance and gather customer feedback on the product line.
  • Analyze sales data and customer feedback to identify areas for improvement.
  • Incorporate customer feedback into future design iterations.

IDEATION WORKSHOP
Summary
Problem
Process
Research
Ideation
Design
Test
Conclusion

The goal of this workshop is to invite 3 type of user profiles to illustrate their workflow by identifying their pain points and proposing solutions.

Drawback of classic workshop

If we want to cover the most of the perspective, it will require more workshops and more participants

Role-playing approach

Role-playing is a way to deeply evaluate a situation that is uncertain so the you can have a full spectrum of how to look at and make sure that you are looking at from all angles.

It is very hard when you are facing a difficult solution yourself as an individual to think of all the possible outcomes and ways to get there. So enlisting other people and sometimes interacting with other people and using those creative brainstorming techniques can be really helpful

Benefits of role-playing in workshop

Time saving: for example with 3 participants (product manager, stylist and modelist) in the workshop. We can take 3 turns for each participant to play different roles and we are able to capture the insights as quick as possible instead of running 3 different workshops separately with one single profile.

Empathy: Role-playing approach also enables different profiles to empathize the tasks of other people and trigger breakthrough solutions.

Virtual reality to streamline the workflow of fashion design

Reduced handovers: VR helps to reduce handovers between process and tools

Centralized space: VR allows team members to consult various materials in a centralized virtual space instead of content hosted in different physical areas where it is time consuming to retrieve and difficult to manage

Specific needs in terms of features for fashion design in VR

Library of models
Version control
Able to conduct 2-ways copy-paste action between public and private models
Workflow validation to reinforce international collaboration

Encouraging people to use VR thanks to multi-user environment usage

The usage of VR by a single user brings biased fear toward the new technology either it is due to technology fear or personal issue

The usage with VR headset and tablet provides a collaborative environment for multiple users to finalize a task

It allows to reduce the awkward moments by individuals, any obstacles can be easily resolved by collective discussion and actions

Image source: LINK
Context

As a trend, commercial mobile phones and tablets have widely implemented the “swipe to trigger contextual action” (SWHIDGET: swipe-revealed hidden widgets) interface to allow shortcut-like action

Issues of lack of clear design guidance for mobile interface design by any commercial product company or operation systems (Ref.):

  • Lack of signifiers makes it unclear where the contextual swipe can be used
  • Content is obscured when the user swipes to reveal actions
  • Some of the actions associated with contextual swipe are nonstandard.
  • Contextual swipe is sometimes used inconsistently within the same app
  • Poorly implemented swipe-to-delete can lead to loss of data
  • Swipe ambiguity can affect how effective contextual swipe is
